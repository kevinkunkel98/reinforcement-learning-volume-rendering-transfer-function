# 2026-10-19: task v3 -- the organs in front, not the skin

Follow-up to [task v2](2026-10-17-task-v2.md). Looking at the viewer's actual
renders (policy + search on ts_s0477), the answers that scored 0.9 looked like
small changes. Three things were wrong; none of them in the learner.

## 1. The unlabelled shell covered half the image

At the v2 start, unlabelled voxels -- skin, fat, unsegmented muscle -- take
up about half of the image, and every organ less than 2 % (val, means over
four volumes, `2026-10-19-scripts/shell_probe.py`):

| | other | soft | skeleton | lungs | liver | heart | kidneys | spleen | vessels |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v2 start | 0.54 | 0.04 | 0.02 | 0.02 | 0.01 | 0.003 | 0.001 | 0.002 | 0.001 |
| unlabelled row without the soft peak | 0.22 | 0.12 | 0.08 | 0.06 | 0.04 | 0.01 | 0.005 | 0.01 | 0.005 |
| no unlabelled row | 0 | 0.16 | 0.11 | 0.07 | 0.05 | 0.01 | 0.007 | 0.01 | 0.007 |

The shell is the soft-tissue peak lighting unlabelled skin and fat by HU
(`transfer.UNLABELLED_PEAKS`); the policy cannot fade it without fading the
soft-tissue class with it. Because attainment is measured on log visibility,
a "more liver" that is complete on paper moves a fraction of a percent of the
image. The new `visible pp` column (`goals.visible_change`: percentage points
of the image moved toward the goal) puts a number on it -- v2, seed 1, 300
episodes, medians:

| v2 | val att | val pp | test att | test pp |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| policy | 0.54 | 0.72 | 0.51 | 0.68 |
| policy + 30 | 0.72 | 0.82 | 0.70 | 0.79 |
| policy + 200 | 0.89 | 1.05 | 0.91 | 0.95 |
| search 200 | 0.66 | 0.71 | 0.65 | 0.61 |

Relative instructions solved at 0.96 move about 0.5 % of the image.

Rendering the same instructions with the unlabelled row's opacity scaled
(no retraining, `2026-10-19-scripts/shell_render.py`) showed 0.1-0.3 bringing the organs forward while keeping the
body outline; at 0 the organs float. **Task v3** holds it at 0.2
(`transfer.UNLABELLED_OPACITY`, set per task by `goals.use_task_version`; the
browser viewer receives it through `/api/state`).

## 2. Brightness on an invisible organ

Brightening a class that is not visible changes nothing on screen (and its
measured brightness is 0 until it shows). v3 samples brightness only for
classes visible at the start (`sample_instruction(visible_brightness=True)`),
falling back to an absolute instruction like the solvable-only sampler does.

## 3. show_only punished hiding things

"Show only the kidneys" on ts_s0477 (first v3 policy + 200) kept the lungs
bright -- 4.6 % → 8.7 % of the image -- at attainment 0.62; with the lungs
hidden the same answer scored 0.43. The unlabelled tissue sees the lungs
peak, so hiding the lungs also took "other" from 24 % to 3 %, which the keep
term charged as drift, and a fully hidden class overshot its −1 target. v3's
show_only asks every hidden class for the visibility floor and lets the
unlabelled tissue fade (`goals.hide_fully`). Re-scored, the search hides the
lungs and soft tissue completely (0.67). v1 and v2 scoring are unchanged.

## Also fixed: every render was upside down

The TotalSegmentator camera (`azimuth 0, elevation 80`) kept VTK's default
+y view-up, which projects onto inferior: heart and lungs were drawn below the
liver and the patient's left on the viewer's left -- in the offscreen renders
and, by the same camera arithmetic and voxel order, in the vtk.js viewer.
`azimuth 180, elevation 80` gives superior up and the patient's right on the
viewer's left (9c24a65). Visibility uses fixed views, so no number changes.

## Training

The v2 recipe unchanged, with `--task-version v3`
(`2026-10-19-scripts/pipeline_v3.sh`): 3000 + 3 × 1500 starts over train +
train_extra, Dyna rounds with balanced closed-loop fine-tuning, three seeds,
about 3.5 hours on the M1. The first v3 run (before fix 3, `out/rl_mb_v3a`)
tracked v2 round for round (round 1 val 0.36 vs 0.34-0.38, closed loop
0.42-0.43 vs 0.44-0.47).

## Debugging with renders (`2026-10-19-scripts/render_episodes.py`)

Every case below was rendered (start | policy | policy + 200) and checked by
eye, not only scored.

**Where the val→test drop came from.** Per test patient, the v3 policy alone
loses most on ts_s0454 (0.74 on v2, 0.39 on v3), a pelvis-only scan: skeleton,
soft tissue and a few vessels, no organs. Its failing episodes were soft-tissue
instructions -- "more soft tissue" scored exactly 0 for every method, "medium
opacity soft tissue" scored −0.01 for a policy answer that visibly brought the
soft tissue in (0 → 2.6 % of the image) and 0.67 for a search answer that
barely did (0.8 %).

**Cause: the ceiling probe.** Absolute levels and the v2 clipping measure a
class against `solo_max`, the most of it a single-peak transfer function
shows -- probed at full peak height only. Visibility is not monotonic in
height: at height 1 the soft-tissue peak also lights the unlabelled muscle and
fat in front of it. Soft tissue shows 11-16 % of the image at height 0.05 and
0.2-2.3 % at height 1 (lungs peak near 0.2):

| ts_s0477, % of image | 0.02 | 0.05 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.4 | 0.7 | 1.0 (old probe) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| soft tissue | 10.3 | 13.6 | 13.0 | 9.8 | 5.5 | 2.7 | 1.5 |
| lungs | 6.5 | 9.7 | 10.7 | 10.5 | 9.5 | 8.2 | 7.3 |
| skeleton | 1.9 | 4.2 | 7.0 | 10.6 | 14.0 | 16.1 | 17.0 |

So on every patient soft tissue's ceiling sat below what the start already
showed: "more soft tissue" was clipped to an empty goal, "high opacity soft
tissue" asked for a tenfold fade, and the policy was trained on
contradictory targets for soft tissue. The policy alone
scored 0.43-0.44 on instructions mentioning soft tissue against 0.52-0.63 on
the rest. **Fix** (v3 only, v1/v2 unchanged): probe heights 0.05-1.0
(`transfer.SOLO_PROBE_HEIGHTS`). No fixed val/test episode is left at zero
start distance (5-6 per split before). The ceiling is also part of the
policy's observation, so v3 was retrained (`out/rl_mb_v3c`).

**Planning through the learned model.** The checkpoint carries its dynamics
ensemble. `rl.model_based.plan` refines the policy's action for one
instruction by gradient descent on the training loss through that model --
no renders, ~0.2 s. On the first v3 seed-2 policy (300 val episodes, old
ceiling):

| | median |
|---|---|
| policy | 0.51 |
| **policy + planning** (100 steps, 16 restarts, beta 1) | **0.66** |
| better of the two by one real evaluation each | 0.70 |
| planning from a no-op instead of the policy | 0.60 |
| 300 steps / 50 steps | 0.61 / 0.61 |
| beta 0.3 / 3 | 0.59 / 0.64 |

More steps exploit the model's errors; starting from the policy matters.
The viewer plans before its search (`server.POLICY_PLAN`).

**Other things the renders show** (not changed):

- Brightness changes on small classes (spleen, liver at < 2 % of the image)
  are not visible even when scored 0.9+.
- An occluder is free to fade: "more heart and less bone" on ts_s0477 also
  took the lungs from 4.6 % to 0.5 %. That is the v2 rule (a class hiding a
  target may fade), but a user may not expect it.
- "A bit less X" on a class at < 1 % of the image changes nothing visible;
  overshooting it to invisible scores −5 (log scale), which only the mean,
  not the median, sees.

## Results after the ceiling fix (`out/rl_mb_v3c`)

Retrained with the same pipeline (`O=out/rl_mb_v3c pipeline_v3.sh`, 4 h 08 on
the M1), evaluated with planning (`eval_v3.sh CKPT TAG SPLIT [--cold] --plan`,
`O=out/rl_mb_v3c/eval COLD=v3c_s0 summarize_v3.py ...`). The ceiling changed
the task, so these numbers compare only with each other, not with the tables
above. Seeds 0 / 1 / 2; the search from the start is one deterministic run.

| | val | test |
|---|---|---|
| policy alone | 0.50 / 0.49 / 0.49 | 0.48 / 0.47 / 0.47 |
| policy + planning (no renders) | 0.62 / 0.63 / 0.63 | 0.62 / 0.65 / 0.64 |
| policy + 30 | 0.67 / 0.66 / 0.67 | 0.67 / 0.67 / 0.66 |
| **policy + planning + 30** | **0.75 / 0.77 / 0.75** | **0.77 / 0.78 / 0.77** |
| policy + 200 | 0.89 / 0.90 / 0.89 | 0.89 / 0.88 / 0.88 |
| policy + planning + 200 | 0.89 / 0.90 / 0.90 | 0.91 / 0.92 / 0.90 |
| search 200 from the start | 0.75 | 0.70 |

Paired Wilcoxon over the 300 episodes, every seed and split:

- planning vs policy alone: p ≤ 2e-7; planning + 30 vs policy + 30:
  p ≤ 3e-5. Planning helps when few renders follow.
- planning + 200 vs policy + 200: p = 0.009-0.87 -- with 200 renders the
  search catches up either way.
- **planning + 30 vs search 200 from the start: p = 0.14-0.9.** Level, not
  better: the policy and its model reach with 30 renders what the search
  needs 200 for. Planning alone (no renders) is below it (p ≤ 7e-5).

The val→test drop is gone (policy alone 0.49-0.50 val, 0.47-0.48 test,
against 0.49-0.51 / 0.43-0.46 before the fix) -- it was the soft-tissue
ceiling on the pelvis-only test patient. The weakest kind is still
show_only (planning + 30: 0.54-0.56 val, 0.48-0.51 test). Renders of seed 1
on 8 val episodes look as asked: "show only the bone and the spleen" now
clears the soft tissue, "low opacity for the soft tissue" fades it.

The viewer runs seed 1, the best on val with planning
(`policy.POLICY_PATH = out/rl_mb_v3c/clb_r3_seed1/checkpoint.pt`).
